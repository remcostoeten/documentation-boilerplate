This folder is also the example of a nested folder: `reference/index.mdx` becomes the folder link, and the pages listed in `reference/meta.json` appear under it while you are inside the folder.

- [Configuration](/docs/components/reference/configuration) documents every option of `site.ts` and `source.config.ts`.
- [Frontmatter](/docs/components/reference/frontmatter) lists the fields a page can set.
- [meta.json](/docs/components/reference/meta-json) lists the fields and entries of a folder file.
- [Environment variables](/docs/components/reference/environment-variables) lists every variable the site reads.
- [Scripts](/docs/components/reference/scripts) lists every `package.json` script.
