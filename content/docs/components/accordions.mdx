## FAQ

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Can I add a page without touching meta.json?">
    Yes. Pages missing from `pages` are appended at the end of their folder, but listing them keeps
    the order explicit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I hide a page from the sidebar?">
    Leave it out of `meta.json` and link to it from another page. It still builds and is still
    searchable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which icons can I use?">
    Any name exported from `src/shared/icons`, such as `Rocket`, `Compass` or `FileText`.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
